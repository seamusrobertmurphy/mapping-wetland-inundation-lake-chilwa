Open water plus water inside the reed beds, 1984 to 2024, labelled by fisher and field evidence. For the coauthor meeting of 27 September 2026, from the manuscript at commit 8f2cc10.

AQuestionwhat is measured, and why it is hard

Surface-water records count only water open to the sky. This study adds water standing within the reeds, which no optical sensor sees and no one can label from above, and labels it with fisher and field evidence.

Hypotheses, Section 1.6

  1. H1 Most surface water stands beneath aquatic vegetation, so open-water records understate inundation.
  2. H2 No reference set drawn from imagery can label the vegetated class. Fisher-derived points can, and they change the mapped extent.
  3. H3 Greenness stays high for weeks to months after water recedes, so index records date a past flood.
Result so farWater within vegetation averaged 130 km², 10 per cent of the combined area, and 19 per cent on the 22 Feb 2012 radar date.
Decide H1 against the numbersH1 expects a majority. Radar gives 10 to 19 per cent, a lower bound because C-band loses water under dense Typha. Agree how the paper reports it.

HISTARFM template

Moreno-Martínez et al. (2020) give monthly 30 m bands B1 to B7, each with its own standard deviation, so the water index carries an error. The study copies this at basin scale, because the product covers only Europe, Somalia and the United States and drops pixels with a vegetation index below −0.1, which removes open water.

Decide Per pixel or basinRebuild per pixel on HISTARFM, giving 30 m maps with an error on every pixel, or keep the basin record with the rebuild as future work, as now written.
Needed Code and labelMove harmonic_fit_2012.py into the manuscript, and fix the 2012 radar labelled ALOS PALSAR.
EModelshow classes and areas are estimated
  1. Feature screen. Murphy et al. (2026) protocol. Separability index M, Shapiro-Wilk, Kruskal-Wallis and Wilcoxon tests, principal components, then a multinomial LASSO with tenfold cross-validation.
  2. Mixture analysis. Four endmembers per sensor era give the share of water, emergent vegetation, dry vegetation and bare soil in each 30 m pixel.
  3. Random forest. 500 trees, four classes, on the 2020 stack of optical, Sentinel-1, PALSAR and mixture layers, split 70 to 30.
  4. State-space model. The Kalman filter of lane D, with a backward pass so later observations improve earlier estimates.
  5. 2012 check. Field points against the harmonic and Envisat maps, on the 30 m grid and the 500 m grid, where a point inside the 1 to 3 km reed belt is rarely mixed.
Decide What the 2012 points testThe harmonic and Envisat maps, as the manuscript now says, or a separate 2012 random forest on Landsat 7.
Needed Feature stackNarrow the random forest to the layers the screen selects.
Done Record fittedStep size 36.5 km² and error scale 0.377 by maximum likelihood. Innovation variance 0.977 against a target of 1.
FScoreshow accuracy is reported

Classes. Confusion matrix, overall accuracy, kappa, producer's and user's accuracy per class, F-score, and McNemar's test between sensor sets, as in Amoakoh et al. (2021).

Time series. Withhold whole dates and predict them. Report RMSE, MAE, bias and R² in km², the RMSE ratio with rejection above 1.1, and Theil's U bias.

Now, Earth Engine through RStudioA Quarto file run in RStudio, R 4.4.1, rendered to Word, 53 R chunks in about 8 minutes. The rgee package sends the image work to Google Earth Engine, so any coauthor can run the whole study from a laptop. Setup and every algorithm are on the back.

Now, Python side scriptsPython 3.12 scripts in 05.scripts/ run the 16-day extraction, harmonic fit, Landsat 7 coverage and 15 m sharpening on Earth Engine, and read Envisat with GDAL. Needed Move them into the manuscript.

Proposed, run locally with sitsThe sits package, 1.5.4 in R, builds image cubes from Digital Earth Africa, Planetary Computer or local GeoTIFFs, with mixture, classification, uncertainty and accuracy tools. It has no harmonic or Kalman fit, so those are written with terra.

Cost of going localEvery clear scene 1984 to 2024 is 152.6 GB, the 2010 to 2014 window 28.5 GB and the 41 annual composites 4.8 GB. Estimated at 38 to 57 working days.

Plain terms

Kalman filterKeeps a running estimate of the lake and corrects it at each observation, trusting reliable views more.

Backward passA second run from the last step to the first, so each estimate also uses later observations.

Harmonic curveA smooth yearly wave of sines and cosines fitted to each pixel and read on any date.

Water indexMNDWI, green minus shortwave infrared over their sum. Water is above zero.

Double bounceA radar pulse bounces off water and then off a stem, and returns bright where reeds stand in water.

RMSEThe typical size of an error, in km², with large errors weighted more.

RMSE ratioError on withheld steps over error on training steps. Above 1.1 the model has fitted noise.

Theil's U biasThe share of error that runs in one direction rather than scattering both ways.

Software and algorithms, back of the sheet

Which program runs each step, which Earth Engine dataset it reads, and which built-in Earth Engine algorithm does the work. Read from the code of Manuscript_2026-08-03.qmd at 8f2cc10 and 05.scripts/.

Can a coauthor run the whole study from RStudio on an ordinary laptop? Yes, while the image work stays on Earth Engine, because Google's servers do the computing and only small tables come back.

What runs where

On Earth Engine. Reading the image archives, cloud masking, compositing, water indices, spectral unmixing, the random forest, its error matrix, and the harmonic regression.

On the laptop. The Quarto document itself, the Kalman filter and backward pass on 936 numbers, terrain routing in WhiteboxTools, tables and figures, and the Envisat radar files.

Proposed. A local sits cube for the 2010 to 2014 window and the annual composites, if the study leaves Earth Engine.
